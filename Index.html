Workforce Analytics & Financial Modeling Framework

Connect-Minute (CM) Architecture

Definition: The cumulative billable telecom duration spent on active customer interactions captured via ACD telephony telemetry (Avaya CMS / Cisco CUIC).

// Telecom Billing Formula

Connect Mins = (Calls Handled × [Talk Time + Hold Time]) / 60
  • Directly tied to switch trunk duration audits.
  • Used for variable on-demand client invoicing models.

Full-Time Equivalent (FTE) Principles

Definition: Standardized unit of productive workload measurement representing one full-time operative's capacity over defined interval windows.

// Mathematical Equations

Net FTE = (Call Volume × AHT in Secs) / Interval Secs
Gross FTE = Net FTE / (1 - Shrinkage %)
  • Net FTE: Pure operational workload without downtime.
  • Gross FTE: Roster requirements incorporating shrinkage (leaves, coaching, downtime).
