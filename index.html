📌 How Capacity Planning Works (Formula & Definition):

• Net Workload Hours: $(\text{Monthly Volume} \times \text{AHT in Sec}) / 3600$

• Net FTE Needed: $\text{Net Workload Hours} / \text{Paid Hours per Month}$

• Gross Required FTE (with Shrinkage & Buffer): $(\text{Net FTE} / (1 - \text{Shrinkage \%})) \times (1 + \text{Buffer \%})$

• Staffing Gap/Surplus: $\text{Current Active HC} - \text{Required Gross FTE}$ (+ve means surplus, -ve means shortage)

📌 How Model Occupancy Works (Formula & Definition):

• Available Staffed Hours: $\text{Headcount} \times \text{Paid Hours} \times (1 - \text{Shrinkage \%})$

• Model Occupancy %: $(\text{Net Workload Hours} / \text{Available Staffed Hours}) \times 100$

• Standard Benchmarks: Optimal range is 75% to 85%. Above 85% causes agent fatigue and burnout; below 75% indicates overstaffing.
