📌 How Capacity Planning Works (Formula & Definition):

• Net Workload Hours: (Monthly Volume × AHT in Sec) / 3600

• Net FTE Needed: Net Workload Hours / Paid Hours per Month

• Gross Required FTE (with Shrinkage & Buffer): (Net FTE / (1 - Shrinkage %)) × (1 + Buffer %)

• Staffing Gap/Surplus: Current Active HC - Required Gross FTE (+ve means surplus, -ve means shortage)

📌 How Model Occupancy Works (Formula & Definition):

• Available Staffed Hours: Headcount × Paid Hours × (1 - Shrinkage %)

• Model Occupancy %: (Net Workload Hours / Available Staffed Hours) × 100

• Standard Benchmarks: Optimal range is 75% to 85%. Above 85% causes agent fatigue and burnout; below 75% indicates overstaffing.
